about

I'm the Technical Lead of FRET, NASA's open-source tool that turns ambiguous, natural-language requirements into precise specifications a machine can check, and I lead the team of engineers and researchers who build it. I'm also a member of the Robust Software Engineering (RSE) Group at NASA Ames Research Center, where I research and build tools for verifying and validating safety-critical systems.

In collaboration with engineers, I apply FRET to help verify complex, often autonomous, aerospace systems before they fly.

I completed my postdoctoral research at Vanderbilt University's Institute for Software Integrated Systems, working under Prof. Janos Sztipanovits. I hold a Doctor of Science from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Joseph Sifakis (2007 Turing Award laureate) and Prof. Simon Bliudze.
